# Element Clash — Game Design Spec (v1)

## 1. Overview

**Players:** 2–6
**Win condition:** First player to reach 50 points, exactly or over, wins immediately.
**Core theme:** Four elements (Water, Earth, Fire, Air) rotate through a cycle of "power." Score cards matching the current power element score better than off-element cards. Event cards let players disrupt opponents or boost themselves, but only during their own element's power turn.

---

## 2. Components

**Total deck: 62 cards**

### 2.1 Score cards (50 cards)
- Values 1–10.
- For each value, 5 copies exist: 1 Water, 1 Earth, 1 Fire, 1 Air, 1 Wild.
- **Wild** cards always count as matching the current power element, regardless of what that element is.

### 2.2 Event cards (12 cards — 3 per element)

| Element | Card |
|---|---|
| Air | Swap |
| Air | Redirect |
| Air | Foresight |
| Earth | Boost |
| Earth | Harvest |
| Earth | Reversal |
| Fire | Discard |
| Fire | Scorch |
| Fire | Ignite |
| Water | Spy |
| Water | Stillness |
| Water | Reflect |

## 3. Setup

1. Shuffle the full 62-card deck.
2. Deal 5 cards to each player (starting hand).
3. Place remaining cards face-down as the **draw pile**.
4. The **youngest player** chooses the starting power element (Water, Earth, Fire, or Air). If age cannot be determined, or two or more players are tied for youngest, the starting player is instead chosen **at random** among the candidates (e.g. a high-card cut or die roll).
5. Play begins with that player. Turn order for the rest of the table follows normal seating order (e.g. clockwise) from that player, per the table's own convention.

---

## 4. The Power Element Cycle

- Fixed cycle order: **Water → Earth → Fire → Air → Water → ...**
- The power element advances **once per full round** — i.e. once every player has taken exactly one turn.
- The power element cycle is **independent of player turn order**. If turn order is reversed (see *Reversal*, §7.2), the power cycle is unaffected and continues in the same fixed sequence.

### 4.1 Turn-order disruption ends the round early

**Redirect** (§7.1) and **Reversal** (§7.2) both disrupt the normal one-turn-per-player pattern that §4 depends on. To keep the power cycle well-defined:

- Playing **Redirect** or **Reversal** ends the current round immediately, whether or not every player has taken a turn yet this round.
- The power element advances immediately at that point, following the fixed cycle order above.
- A new round begins immediately afterward. The turn on which Redirect or Reversal was played counts as **the first turn of the new round**.
- Any further turns generated by the same card (e.g. Reversal's immediate extra turn, or the extra turn Redirect creates in a 2-player game) are simply the next turns of this new round — they do not trigger a second round-end.
- No other event card ends a round early. Only Redirect and Reversal do this.

---

## 5. Turn Structure

Each turn, in strict order:

1. **Draw** exactly 1 card from the draw pile.
2. **Play** exactly 1 card from hand — either:
   - a **score card** (see §6), or
   - an **event card**, if legal to play (see §7).
3. **Turn ends.** Any cards not played remain in hand. There is no hand size limit.

**No legal play available:** Since any score card is legal on any turn (§6), this can only happen if a player's entire hand is event cards that don't match the current power element. In that case, the player **discards 1 card of their choice** from hand instead of playing, and their turn ends. No score is earned and no event effect occurs that turn.

---

## 6. Scoring

Any score card may be played on any turn, regardless of the current power element — there are no restrictions on which score cards are legal to play.

**Resolution when a score card is played:**

- **If the card's element matches the current power element:**
  Points scored = face value + (half of face value, rounded up).
- **If it does not match:**
  Points scored = face value only.
- **Wild cards always match**, and therefore always receive the bonus.

### 6.1 Bonus reference table

| Face value | Bonus (½ rounded up) | Total if matched | Total if unmatched |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| 3 | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 | 6 | 4 |
| 5 | 3 | 8 | 5 |
| 6 | 3 | 9 | 6 |
| 7 | 4 | 11 | 7 |
| 8 | 4 | 12 | 8 |
| 9 | 5 | 14 | 9 |
| 10 | 5 | 15 | 10 |

### 6.2 Win condition

The moment any player's cumulative score reaches **50 or more**, the game ends immediately and that player wins.

**Ties at 50+ cannot occur.** Only one player's cumulative score can change on any given turn — either by playing a score card (§6) or by Boost recalculating their own previously-scored card (§7.2). Since turns are strictly sequential (§5, §7 Resolution order), no two players' scores can cross 50 at the same moment. No tiebreaker rule is needed.

---

## 7. Event Cards

**General rule:** An event card is only legal to play during its own element's power turn. There are no exceptions to this rule.

**Resolution order:** Only one card is played per turn (§5), and turns proceed one player at a time in strict turn order — there is no true simultaneity. Within a round, if the round's power element gives several players an event card they could play, each player's event card is played and **fully resolves** before the next player's turn begins. "Simultaneous" event cards never occur; the apparent case (multiple players holding the same round's element) is just normal sequential turn order.

### 7.1 Air

**Swap**
- Legal only when Air has power.
- The player choose one card from their own hand (fully visible to them).
- The player then chooses a positional slot (e.g. "3rd card from the left") from a target player's hand, **without seeing its value**.
- The chosen slot **must exist** — i.e. the target player's hand must have at least that many cards. If it does not, that slot is not a legal choice; the player must pick a different slot or a different target. Swap cannot be played at all if no target player has any card in hand (should not occur in practice, since every player holds at least their post-draw card).
- The two chosen cards swap hands.

**Redirect**
- Legal only when Air has power.
- Skips the next player's turn entirely; play passes straight to the player after them.
- In a 2-player game, this effectively grants the playing player two consecutive turns, since the only opponent is skipped and play returns immediately.
- Ends the current round immediately and advances the power element — see §4.1.

**Foresight**
- Legal only when Air has power.
- Look at the top 3 cards of the draw pile.
- Reorder them in any order the player chooses.
- Return them to the top of the draw pile in the new order.

### 7.2 Earth

**Boost**
- Legal only when Earth has power.
- Playing Boost **is itself the player's one play for the turn**. It must target the score card that player played on their **immediately preceding turn** — the last score card they placed before this Boost. Boost cannot target a future play, a card in hand, or another player's card.
- If that player's immediately preceding turn was not a score-card play (e.g. they played an event card, or this is their first turn), Boost has no legal target and cannot be played.
- That card's scoring value becomes **face value + 5, capped at a maximum of 10**.
- The match/no-match bonus rule (§6) still applies on top of this adjusted value.
- The targeted card has **already scored** at its original value on the turn it was played. Boost recalculates its score using the new boosted value and the bonus rule, and adds the **difference** (new total minus original total) to the player's cumulative score. It does not re-add the full new total.

**Harvest**
- Legal only when Earth has power.
- Draw 2 additional cards immediately.

**Reversal**
- Legal only when Earth has power.
- **Permanently** reverses the direction of player turn order for the remainder of the game.
- The playing player takes an **immediate extra turn** right now, in addition to the direction change.
- This extra turn is **an extra play only** — the player plays 1 more card from their current hand. There is **no extra draw**; the normal §5 turn structure (draw 1, then play 1) does not apply to this bonus play.
- Ends the current round immediately and advances the power element — see §4.1. (This replaces the earlier v1 wording that said Reversal does not affect the power cycle — it does.)

### 7.3 Fire

**Discard**
- Legal only when Fire has power.
- Every player **except** the one who played this card must discard 1 card of their own choosing from their hand.

**Scorch**
- Legal only when Fire has power.
- Choose a target player.
- The next event card that player attempts to play this round is cancelled and discarded instead of taking effect.
- If the round ends (§4 / §4.1) before that player attempts to play an event card, Scorch's effect **lapses and does nothing**. It does not carry over into a later round.

**Ignite**
- Legal only when Fire has power.
- Choose a target opponent.
- The next score card that opponent plays this round scores at **half face value, rounded down** — this replaces normal scoring entirely; the power-match bonus does not apply to that card.
- If the round ends (§4 / §4.1) before that opponent plays a score card, Ignite's effect **lapses and does nothing**. It does not carry over into a later round.

### 7.4 Water

**Spy**
- Legal only when Water has power.
- Choose a target player.
- Reveal that player's entire hand to you (and only you).

**Stillness**
- Legal only when Water has power.
- Choose a target player.
- On that player's next turn, they must draw a card and **immediately discard it**, skipping their play action for that turn entirely.

**Reflect**
- Legal only when Water has power.
- Choose a target player.
- The next event card that target player plays **against you** this round is cancelled, and its effect is redirected to target its own caster instead.
- If the round ends (§4 / §4.1) before that player plays an event card against you, Reflect's effect **lapses and does nothing**. It does not carry over into a later round.

---

## 8. Draw Pile Exhaustion

If the draw pile is ever emptied and a player needs to draw:

1. Shuffle the entire discard pile — this includes played event cards, cards discarded via any effect, **and** score cards that have already been played and scored.
2. The reshuffled pile becomes the new draw pile.
3. **Points already scored are permanent** and belong to the player who scored them. A scored card re-entering the draw pile does not remove or transfer the points it already earned.

---

## 9. Card Interaction Notes

- **Wild + Ignite:** If a Wild card is targeted by Ignite, it still scores at half face value, rounded down — Ignite overrides the match bonus entirely, so Wild's "always matches" property is irrelevant in this case.
- **Boost + power mismatch:** Boost changes a card's *value*, not its *element*. A boosted card that doesn't match the current power element still only scores its (boosted) face value, with no bonus.
- **Scorch vs. instant-effect events:** Scorch cancels the *next* event card a target attempts to play. It does not retroactively cancel one already resolved.

## 10. Open Items / Not Yet Specified

All items from the original v1 draft have been resolved:

- **No legal play available:** settled in §5 (forced discard of 1 card, turn ends).
- **Simultaneous power-turn events:** settled in §7 (Resolution order — turns are always strictly sequential, no true simultaneity).
- **Tie at 50+:** settled in §6.2 (proven impossible under these rules — no tiebreaker needed).
- **"Youngest player" fallback:** settled in §3 step 4 (random selection among tied/unknown-age candidates).

None remain open as of this revision.
